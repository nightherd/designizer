01 — The place

We opened in 2019 after Marta got tired of teaching in rented rooms with someone else's furniture.

The studio is a former seamstress's workshop. We kept the tall windows, the tiled stove nobody can use, and the slightly sloping floor, which you will notice in tree pose and then stop noticing.

We keep classes small on purpose. Twelve people means the teacher actually sees you, and nobody has to be anonymous in the back row. No mirrors, no playlists with lyrics, no incense. There's tea afterwards.

03 — What we teach

Five kinds of class, none of them mysterious

Vinyasa All levels · 60 min

Breath-linked movement with a clear shape. Mornings are gentler, Tuesday evenings with Dara get sweaty. Modifications are always offered, never announced.

Hatha Beginners welcome · 75 min

Fewer poses held longer, with plenty of explanation. If you've never done yoga, start with Inês on a Tuesday.

Yin Evenings · 75 min

Floor-based, long holds, props everywhere. Less stretching than it sounds, more lying down than you'd expect. Good after a bad week.

Iyengar-ish Some experience · 90 min

Joaquim trained in the Iyengar tradition and teaches a looser version. Blocks, belts, walls, and a lot of attention to alignment.

Nidra Thursday night · 45 min

Guided rest, lying down under a blanket. People fall asleep. That's fine, that's allowed.

Lunch stretch 30 min

For people who work nearby and have a laptop-shaped back. Come in your office clothes. We'll have you out by 13:05.

04 — Teachers

Marta Figueiredo

Former architect. Teaches like she's drawing a floor plan: where you are, where you're going, and the shortest honest route.

Inês Calado

Hatha, Nidra

Physiotherapist by day. Calm, funny, will correct your wrists before you know they're wrong. The one to see if you're new.

Twenty years on the mat, from Pune to Porto. Precise and patient. He owns more blocks than anyone should.
