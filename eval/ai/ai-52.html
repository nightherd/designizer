What we mean by natural

There's no legal definition, so here is ours. Every wine we pour meets all four.

  • Farmed clean

    Organic or biodynamic vineyards, picked by hand. No synthetic sprays.

  • Wild yeast

    Fermented with the yeasts that came in on the grapes, not from a packet.

  • Nothing added

    No added sugar, acid, oak chips or colour. Not fined, not filtered, so some bottles are cloudy.

  • Low sulphur

    None at all, or a tiny dose at bottling. Under 30mg per litre either way.
