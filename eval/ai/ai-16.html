When the stages are open

Schacht is the main stage at the foot of the heap. Kohlenbunker is the indoor room in the old coal bunker. Gipfel is the small sound system at the summit, which plays slow music for sunrise.

Tickets

Every ticket gets you onto the heap and into all three stages. Prices include booking fees and a free regional transport pass (VRR) from Friday morning to Monday noon.

Under-18s can come in with a guardian on the day tickets only.

Getting there

By tram

Take tram 301 from Gelsenkirchen Hbf to Buer Rathaus, then the festival shuttle bus. Shuttles run every 10 minutes, all night.

By train

Gelsenkirchen Hbf is 25 minutes from Düsseldorf and 15 from Essen. Your ticket covers regional trains across the VRR network.

By bike

The Emscher cycle path runs past the gate. There's a guarded bike park with 2,000 spaces, free for ticket holders.

On the heap

The climb to Gipfel takes about 15 minutes on gravel paths. Wear proper shoes. A step-free route runs on the east side.

Before you come

Can I leave and come back in?

Yes. Your wristband lets you in and out as often as you like, until 4am each night.

Is the site accessible?

Schacht and Kohlenbunker are step-free, with a raised viewing platform at Schacht. Gipfel is reached by a step-free path that takes about 25 minutes. Book an access ticket and a companion goes free.

What happens if it rains?

The festival goes ahead. Kohlenbunker is indoors, and Schacht has a covered area for 3,000 people. We only close Gipfel if there's lightning.

Can I get a refund?

You can resell your ticket at face value through our exchange until 14 May. If the festival is cancelled, you get a full refund automatically.
