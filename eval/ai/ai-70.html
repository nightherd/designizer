    What happens when the grid drops

    Most of our customers sleep through it. Here's the sequence, from the moment a tree hits the line on your road.

    1. 0 sec

      The grid goes down

      The battery's gateway detects the voltage loss and disconnects your house from the street, so no power flows back onto lines that crews are repairing.

    2. < 1 sec

      Your house switches over

      Lights may blink once. Clocks, routers and the furnace controller keep going. Nothing to pull-start, nothing to refuel.

    3. Hours

      You keep living normally

      The app shows how much charge is left and which circuits are drawing power. You can shed loads, like the dryer, to stretch it further.

    4. Morning

      Solar tops it up

      If you have panels, they keep charging the battery during the outage, even with the grid down.

    5. Restore

      The grid comes back

      The gateway reconnects your house automatically, then recharges the battery so it's ready for the next storm.

    From first call to switched on

    Usually six to ten weeks, most of it waiting on permits and the utility. We handle that paperwork.

    1. Site visit

      An electrician looks at your panel, meter and wall space, and asks which circuits matter most to you.

      About an hour, free

    2. Design and quote

      A fixed price and a one-line diagram showing exactly what will be backed up. No sales follow-up calls.

      Within a week

    3. Permits and utility

      We file the electrical permit and the interconnection application with your utility, and enroll you in any battery program it offers.

      3 to 8 weeks

    4. Install day

      Two electricians, one day. Power is off for about three hours. We walk you through the app before we leave.

      1 day
