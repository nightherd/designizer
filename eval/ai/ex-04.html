How it works

From idea to ownership
in three steps

01 / CONNECT

Link your wallet

Use MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet or WalletConnect. We never hold your keys, and you sign every transaction yourself.

02 / MINT

Mint your work

Upload art, audio or video, set supply and royalties, and mint an ERC-721 or ERC-1155 token. Gas is paid in ETH or on Base for pennies.

03 / TRADE

List, bid & collect

Sell at a fixed price or open a timed auction. Settlement is instant and creators earn a royalty on every future resale.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a crypto wallet to browse?

No. You can explore every collection, view listings and follow artists without connecting a wallet. You only need one when you buy, bid, mint or sell.

Which networks do you support?

Ethereum mainnet and Base today, with Polygon and Arbitrum on the roadmap. Each listing shows its network so you always know where the asset lives.

What does Prismarket charge?

A flat 2.5% fee on successful sales. Minting and listing are free. Network gas is the only other cost, and you see it before you sign.

How do I get paid as a creator?

Primary sales settle to your wallet instantly. Secondary royalties are paid automatically on every resale, and you can split them between collaborators.
