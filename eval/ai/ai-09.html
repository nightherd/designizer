Common questions

Can I skip or pause a delivery?

Yes. Skip any week from your account before the Monday cutoff. Pausing keeps your plan and your dog's profile saved.

How do I switch my dog over?

Most dogs switch in about a week. Start with a quarter new food, mixed into the old, and add a little more each day.

Does it stay cold on the doorstep?

Boxes ship in insulated liners with ice packs. Move the food to the fridge or freezer as soon as it arrives.

What if my dog won't eat it?

Tell us within 14 days and we will refund the first box, no return needed.
