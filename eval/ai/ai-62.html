No wheat, barley or rye has ever come through our door.

One kitchen, one rule.We don’t have a gluten-free corner. The whole bakery is gluten-free, so there’s no shared oven, bench or flour dust.

Every delivery is checked.We buy from mills that don’t process wheat, and we keep the ingredient labels on file for anyone who wants to see them.

Ask us anything.Allergies beyond gluten, such as nuts, dairy and eggs, are listed on every item at the counter.
