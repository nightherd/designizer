How we run things

Four promises, written down so we can be held to them.

  1. Your list leaves with you.

    One click exports every subscriber, every payment status and every issue you’ve sent as plain files. Paid readers move with you too — we hand over the card relationships, not just the email addresses.

  2. We don’t sell your readers to other writers.

    There’s no “recommended for you” sidebar, no network effect we’re secretly optimising for. If a reader finds you, it’s because someone shared you, which is how it used to work and how it should.

  3. The email looks like you wrote it.

    Plain, fast, readable on a cracked phone screen. Footnotes, pull quotes, images that don’t get clipped by Gmail. Your own domain from day one, at no extra charge.

  4. One fee. It never goes up on you.

    5% of what you earn, plus the card processor’s cut. The rate you sign up on is the rate you keep, written into your terms. No “pro tier”, no per-subscriber surcharges at 10,000.

Questions people actually ask
How do you make money if you don’t promote anything?

The 5%. That’s it. It means we only do well when writers do well, which is a constraint we chose on purpose. We’re profitable at around 3,000 earning writers and we passed that in March.

Can I write for free and never charge anyone?

Yes. Free newsletters are free forever, with no subscriber cap. We’d rather you were here when you eventually decide to charge.

What happens to my list if Quire shuts down?

We keep a nightly export of every account ready to download, and our terms commit us to 90 days’ notice and a full paid-subscriber handover to a processor of your choice. We’d like that never to matter.

Do you have a podcast feature / community forum / app?

No, no, and no. Email and the web, done carefully. If that changes, it’ll be because writers asked, not because a competitor shipped one.

I’m coming from somewhere else. How painful is the move?

Usually an afternoon. Upload your subscriber export, connect your payments account, and we’ll bring your archive across with formatting intact. If anything breaks, write to us and a person will help.
