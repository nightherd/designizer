What we do differently

Fewer people. More time in each place.

i.

Small enough to talk to

Eight people is the most we will take on a trip. Small enough that the guide learns your name by the second day, and the ryokan staff can actually accommodate a dietary request.

ii.

Guides from where you're going

Our leads are based in Kyoto, Sendai, Kanazawa and Naha. They are chosen for local knowledge, not for reciting a script, and they will tell you where they eat on their day off.

iii.

Slow days on purpose

Each itinerary keeps at least one unscheduled afternoon in every city. Free time is part of the plan, not something left over from it.

A sample week · Kyoto to the Coast

What a day actually looks like.

Mornings are for the things that are best before the crowds. Afternoons are yours. Evenings are often shared, around one long table.

01

Arrive Kyoto, walk Higashiyama

Meet your guide at the hotel for a slow evening stroll through Gion, ending with dinner in a small kaiseki counter.

03

Fushimi Inari before sunrise

Walk the torii tunnels with the first trains and the morning light. Breakfast is wherever your guide's cousin runs a bakery.

05

Train north to the Sea of Japan

A limited express ride, then a free afternoon in Kanazawa's old geisha district. Dinner is at a fishmonger's counter, with the fish cut that morning.

07

Noto Peninsula at its own pace

Salt-kiln workshop, a coastal drive with stops whenever someone says stop, and a night in a family-run inn with its own onsen.

Questions

Before you book.

Anything not answered here, write to us and a real person will reply within a working day.

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Your guide handles restaurant ordering, train platforms, and anything that requires a phone call. We will send a short phrase sheet, but it is there for fun, not survival.

What happens if I travel alone?

Most of our guests do. You are matched with a room of your own at no single supplement on our shared-room departures, and a roommate is only assigned if you ask for one.

How physically demanding are the tours?

Each tour page lists its pace. Expect 6 to 10 km of walking on most days, with stairs at temples and shrines. We can arrange seated alternatives at many sites on request.

Is travel insurance included?

No, but we strongly recommend a policy that covers trip cancellation and medical care abroad. We will provide a list of providers that cover Japan.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, halal and most allergy requirements are workable with notice. Please tell us early; some ryokan kitchens need a few weeks to plan.
