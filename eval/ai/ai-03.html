New patients

Your first visit, step by step

Most people are in and out in about 60 minutes. Bring a photo ID and your insurance card.

  1. Book online or call

    Pick a time that works. We will confirm by text or email.

  2. Forms & insurance

    Fill out health history online ahead of time. We verify your benefits for you.

  3. Exam & X-rays

    Your dentist reviews your teeth, gums, and bite, and talks through what they see.

  4. Your plan

    You get a written treatment plan with costs. Nothing starts until you say yes.

Questions

Good to know

Do you see children under age 3?

Yes. We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth. Our visits for little ones are short and focused on building comfort.

Do you treat dental anxiety?

Absolutely. We offer nitrous oxide, oral sedation for longer procedures, and a "stop anytime" approach where you control the pace. Let us know when you book.

How often should I get a cleaning?

Most people do well every six months. Some patients with gum disease or higher risk for cavities may need every three or four months. We will tell you what fits your mouth.

What if I do not have dental insurance?

Our in-house membership plan covers two cleanings, two exams, and X-rays each year for a flat monthly fee. We also offer self-pay rates and payment plans for larger treatment.
