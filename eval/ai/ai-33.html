02 — Process

Four steps, and no surprise on the final invoice.

Most freight problems start with a missing document or a wrong classification. We check both before the cargo leaves the dock.

  1. 1

    Brief

    We ask the questions that move money: HS codes, Incoterms, dimensions, gross weight and the date the goods are ready.

    Same day
  2. 2

    Quote

    One rate, all-in. Origin charges, freight, surcharges and destination fees are listed separately, with the assumptions stated.

    Within 4 hours
  3. 3

    Book and file

    We confirm space, issue the booking and prepare the bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list and origin certificate.

    Documents checked twice
  4. 4

    Deliver and close

    You get live milestones, proof of delivery and a duty reconciliation. Any variance from the quote is explained line by line.

    Closed within 5 days
