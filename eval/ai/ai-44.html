Questions parents ask

What if I forget my master password?

Use the recovery kit you printed at setup, or ask a family admin to approve a reset. We can't reset it for you because we never have it.

Can my partner see everything I save?

No. Each person has a private vault. Only logins you choose to share show up for others.

Does it work if my family uses different phones?

Yes. Latchkey runs on iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux and as a browser extension for Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.

What happens when the trial ends?

You pick a plan or your vault becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you can export everything whenever you like.

Can I move over from my current password manager?

Yes. Import a file from 1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, Dashlane, Chrome or Apple Passwords in a couple of minutes.
