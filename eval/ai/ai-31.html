03 — Process

What happens after you send a file.

i.

We read it first

Before quoting, someone actually opens your document. If it's outside what we know, we tell you and suggest who might.

within 2 hours
ii.

A fixed quote

One number, one date. No “from” prices after the fact, no surprise formatting fees for PDFs.

same day
iii.

Translate, then revise

A second translator compares every sentence to the source. Questions go to you in one list, not twelve emails.

as quoted
iv.

Delivered, with notes

You get the file in its original format, plus a short note on any choices you should know about.

+ 30 days of free fixes
