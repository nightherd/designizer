Questions

Is Stillwater a replacement for therapy?

No. Stillwater is a self-reflection tool, not medical care or a substitute for professional help. Many people use it alongside therapy.

Do I have to write every day?

Not at all. There are no streaks to lose. Reminders are optional and you control the schedule.

Who can read my entries?

Only you. Entries are encrypted on your device, and we can't read them.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Write anywhere, and your entries sync securely when you're back online.
