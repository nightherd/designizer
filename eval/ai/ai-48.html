The studio

A small studio with a long attention span.

We make films about people who are rarely the subject of a headline: nurses, fishers, archivists, and the families who keep a town running.

Ember & Lantern is a team of directors, producers, editors and researchers based in Lisbon and Toronto. We take on a small number of projects each year so that every film gets a full crew, a proper edit and the time to find its real shape.

We work with broadcasters, streamers, foundations and independent funders, and we keep creative control where it matters most: the story itself.

How we work

Patient by design.

Our films take time because the best moments rarely happen on a schedule. This is the path most of our projects follow.

  1. 01

    Listen first

    We begin with conversations, not cameras. Research, meetings and a short paper that tests whether the story holds.

  2. 02

    Earn trust on location

    Scouting and pre-production with subjects and their communities. We agree on boundaries before we roll.

  3. 03

    Shoot what happens

    Lean crews, long takes and room for the unexpected. We keep the camera close and the footprint small.

  4. 04

    Find the shape in the edit

    Many months in the edit room, testing cuts with trusted audiences until the film knows what it is saying.

  5. 05

    Carry it into the world

    Premieres, screenings, and outreach that puts the film in front of the people who can act on it.
