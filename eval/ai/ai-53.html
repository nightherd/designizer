Questions landlords ask first

Do I lose control of my property?

No. You set the minimum rent, approve every tenant and every repair above your limit. We act inside the limits you give us.

What if my tenant stops paying?

We send notices on day 3 and day 7, start the legal process on your approval, and keep you updated each step. Our eviction coordination is included. Court fees are not.

Can I manage some units myself?

Yes. Many owners start with one or two properties and add more once they trust the statements.

Where do you operate?

Twelve metro areas across the Upper Midwest. Enter your postcode below and we'll confirm coverage within a business day.

Is my money kept separate from yours?

Yes. Rent and deposits sit in trust accounts held in your name, audited every year.
