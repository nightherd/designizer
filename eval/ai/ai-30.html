Why go solar

A smarter, steadier way to power your home.

Rising utility rates are not going away. Solar gives you control over a meaningful share of your energy spending for decades.

Lower monthly bills

Most of our customers offset the majority of their electricity use from day one, with savings that compound as utility rates rise.

Higher home value

Owned solar systems are widely associated with higher resale value, and they make your home easier to sell as an energy-efficient property.

Energy independence

Generate your own power on your own roof, and protect your household from future price spikes and supply disruptions.

Cleaner footprint

A typical residential system can prevent several tons of carbon emissions per year compared with grid electricity from fossil fuels.

How it works

From first call to first kilowatt in four steps.

  1. Free assessment

    An engineer visits your home, reviews your roof, shading, and usage history, and maps out the best layout.

  2. Custom design

    After utility approval, your system goes live. Track production anytime from your phone with our app.

Questions

Everything you want to know before going solar.

Can't find your answer? Our energy advisors are happy to talk it through.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installs take 1 to 2 days on-site. Permitting and utility approval typically add a few weeks before your system is switched on.

Will solar work on my roof?

Most pitched and flat roofs in good condition are suitable. During your free assessment we check roof age, orientation, shading, and structural capacity.

What happens on cloudy days or at night?

Solar panels still produce power on cloudy days, though at reduced output. At night, you draw from the grid as usual. Net metering credits your excess daytime production back to your account where available.

What incentives are available?

Many homeowners qualify for federal tax credits and state or utility rebates. Your proposal will show the incentives that apply to your address.

Do I need to replace my roof first?

If your roof is near the end of its life, we recommend replacing it before installing panels, since removing them later costs extra. We can coordinate with a roofing partner.

What maintenance does a system need?

Very little. Panels are self-cleaning in most climates, and we recommend an annual inspection. Your monitoring app will alert you if production drops unexpectedly.
