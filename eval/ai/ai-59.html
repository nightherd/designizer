The problem

Enterprise ATS tools assume you have a recruiting team.

11

tabs open the last time we watched a founder fill one candidate scorecard.

The big platforms were designed for a company with a recruiting ops manager, a compliance review, and a procurement process. You have a Notion table, a Gmail label called "candidates?", and a sinking feeling that someone good went quiet two weeks ago.

Rung is what's left when you remove everything that only matters at 500 people. A pipeline you can read at a glance, a shared inbox that files itself, and a nudge when someone's been sitting too long.

We built it for our own hiring at two previous startups. It got used for the third one, then for our friends', and then we stopped pretending it was a side project.

What it does

Five things. We're fairly sure that's enough.

01

One pipeline per role

Drag people between stages, or just reply to the email thread and Rung moves them. Stages are yours to rename; the default is three because three is what most of you use.

02

Inbox that files itself

Forward anything to jobs@yourco.rung.app or connect Gmail. Resumes get parsed, duplicates get merged, and the thread stays attached to the person.

03

Scorecards in 90 seconds

Four questions, a 1–5, and a free-text box. Interviewers can't see each other's scores until they've submitted their own, which cuts the "well, Sam liked her" effect.

04

Quiet-candidate alerts

Anyone who has sat in a stage longer than you said they would gets flagged on Monday morning. That's the whole notification system. We don't ping you at 9pm.

05

A careers page you don't have to design

Hosted at yourco.rung.app/jobs or embedded on your site with a single script tag. It looks like a page a person wrote, not a widget.

Deliberately missing

The features we refused to build.

  • AI candidate scoring. You can read a resume. We'd rather not rank humans with a number we can't explain.
  • Seven-step approval workflows. You have a Slack channel.
  • Per-seat pricing. Your whole team should see the pipeline, including the person who started on Monday.
  • "Talent community" newsletters. Nobody wants them. Least of all candidates.
  • Onboarding consultants. If it takes a call to set up, we built it wrong.
  • Lock-in. Export everything as CSV and PDFs whenever you like, including after you cancel.

Questions

Things people ask before they sign up.

Can I move over from Notion, Sheets, or Greenhouse?

Yes. Drop a CSV or a Greenhouse export on the import screen. It maps the columns for you and shows what it'll do before it does it.

What happens when we hit 50 people?

It keeps working. You might want something heavier at some point, and when you do, the export is one click. We'd rather you leave happy than stay stuck.

Is candidate data safe?

Hosted in the EU on encrypted storage, with GDPR deletion requests handled from the candidate's own profile. A data processing agreement is available without needing to ask a salesperson.

Who's behind this?

Two people, Marta and Joe, who've run hiring at three startups between them and got tired of the tools. You can email us at hello@rung.app. It really is us.
