How it works

Four steps to real self-custody.

Generate

Create your seed on the device using hardware entropy. It never leaves the secure element.

Back up

Write your 24 words on the included steel-ready card. Store it somewhere safe and offline.

Receive

Verify your receive address on Ember's screen before sending any funds to it.

Sign

Build a transaction in your wallet app, scan it, review it on-device, and approve.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What if I lose my Ember?

Your bitcoin is safe. Restore your 24-word backup onto a new Ember or any BIP39-compatible wallet and your funds are right where you left them.

Why Bitcoin-only?

Less code means a smaller attack surface. Focusing on a single asset lets us audit, test, and perfect the things that matter for storing bitcoin.

Can I trust the supply chain?

Ember ships in tamper-evident packaging and verifies its own firmware on first boot. Because builds are reproducible, you can independently confirm what is running on your device.

Does it work with my wallet software?

Yes. Ember uses standard PSBT files and QR codes, so it works with most wallet apps that support them, including Sparrow and Nunchuk.

What happens if the company disappears?

Everything is open source and your seed is standard BIP39. You are never locked in to our software or our servers.
