The studio

We started in 2016 with one secondhand wheel and a kiln that only worked if you kicked it. Today there are six wheels, a glaze room that smells like wet stone, and a shelf of mugs that didn't make it — we keep them to remind new students that everyone's first twenty pots are wonky.

Classes are run by working potters, not instructors who read the manual last night. You'll centre clay badly, then less badly, and by week three someone will say "oh, that one's nice" and mean it.

— Marguerite & Tobias, who run the place

From lump to cup

What happens to your clay after class

Week 1–2

Throw

You shape the piece on the wheel or by hand while the clay is soft and forgiving.

A few days

Dry & trim

Pieces firm up to leather-hard. We trim feet and add handles before they get too stiff.

Overnight

Bisque fire

A first firing to 1000°C turns clay into ceramic. It's fragile, chalky, and ready for glaze.

Two weeks later

Glaze & fire

You pick colours, we fire to 1240°C, and it comes out different from how you imagined. Usually better.

(yes, some things crack. it's part of it.)

Before you ask

Questions people send us

Do I need any experience?

None. Most of our Wheel Basics students have never touched clay. Hand Building is even gentler if you want to ease in.

What should I wear?

Clothes that can take a splash of slip, and closed shoes. Short nails help. We have aprons, but they don't always win.

When do I get my pots back?

Usually two to three weeks after your final class, once everything has been glaze fired. We'll message you when they're ready to collect.

What if I miss a week?

You can join one of the other groups that week, space permitting. Your clay will wait for you under plastic.

Can I buy a gift voucher?

Yes. Choose any course, or a plain dollar amount. Vouchers are valid for a year and come as a printed card with a very small hand-stamped pot.

Is the studio accessible?

The ground floor entrance is step-free, but the studio itself is up one flight. Email us and we'll arrange a lower-level wheel if needed.
