02 — Grading

Four grades. No adjectives that mean nothing.

Unrestored
Untouched since it left the factory, with its original dial, hands and case finish. Rare, and priced accordingly. Marks and wear are described, not hidden.
Good
Working and complete. Expect visible wear on the case, a refinished or replaced crystal, and at least one non-original part. Listed on the bench with each substitution named.
Very good
Light wear, original dial and hands, and a service within the last three years. The grade most of our stock sits in.
Excellent
Close to the day it was sold. No refinishing, a clean dial, and documented timing within tolerance. Very few pieces reach this grade.
