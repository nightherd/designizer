FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Our support team is real people, based in the US, and they answer fast.

Can I switch to Hearth in the middle of the year?

Yes. We import your year-to-date payroll history from your previous provider so your tax filings and W-2s stay accurate. Our onboarding team handles the migration at no extra cost.

What happens if there's a tax filing mistake?

If we make an error on a filing, we'll fix it and cover any resulting penalties and interest. That's our accuracy guarantee, included on every plan.

Do you support contractors as well as employees?

Absolutely. Pay contractors by direct deposit, collect W-9s during onboarding, and we'll generate and file 1099-NEC forms automatically at year end.

How fast do employees get paid?

Standard direct deposit arrives in two business days. Core and Plus plans include next-day deposit, so you can run payroll later in the week.

Is my data secure?

Hearth uses bank-level 256-bit encryption, SOC 2 Type II audited infrastructure, and two-factor authentication for every account.
