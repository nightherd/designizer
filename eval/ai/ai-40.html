02 — What we do

Four things, done properly, every month.

We don’t do payroll for restaurants or audits for nonprofits. Staying narrow is why we can tell you what a Walmart “WFS reimbursement” line is without looking it up.

A.

Monthly bookkeeping & close

Books closed by the 10th business day. Every channel’s payouts broken into sales, fees, refunds, tax and reserves, matched against the bank.

  • Payout-level reconciliation for every channel
  • Accrual-basis P&L, balance sheet, cash flow
  • Ad spend and app subscriptions categorised
  • A short written note on what changed and why
B.

Inventory & cost of goods

Landed cost that includes freight, duties and 3PL fees, so your gross margin is a number you can actually price against.

  • Landed cost per SKU, updated per PO
  • Month-end inventory valuation and shrink
  • Syncs with Cin7, Katana, Shopify inventory
  • Deposits to suppliers tracked, not expensed
C.

Sales tax

We figure out where you have nexus, get you registered, and file the returns. Marketplace-facilitated sales separated so you don’t pay twice.

  • Annual nexus study across all 45 taxing states
  • Registrations and filings, monthly or quarterly
  • Voluntary disclosure if you’re behind
  • Notice handling. Forward us the letter.
D.

Income tax & planning

Business and owner returns prepared by the same people who keep your books, so nobody in March is guessing what happened in July.

  • 1120-S, 1065 and personal returns
  • Quarterly estimates and a mid-year projection
  • 1099-K and Form 1099 reconciliation
  • S-corp salary and entity structure advice

08 — Questions

Things people ask before signing.

Do I have to switch from QuickBooks or Xero?

No. We work in both. If you’re on something else, we’ll talk about it, but we won’t force a migration in month one.

Do you use A2X, Link My Books, or something else?

Usually A2X for Shopify and Amazon settlements, and our own workpapers for the channels it doesn’t cover. The connector subscription is included in your fee.

My books are a year behind. Is that a problem?

It’s common. We’ll quote a fixed-price catch-up, usually finished in three to six weeks, then roll straight into monthly work. If you owe back sales tax, we’ll tell you before we start, not after.

What if I want to leave?

Thirty days’ notice. You keep your accounting file, every workpaper, and every login. We’ll do a handoff call with whoever’s next.

Who actually does the work?

A senior accountant who owns your account, with a staff accountant doing the reconciliations. Same two people every month. Everyone is a US-based employee of the firm.

Can you help if I sell outside the US?

We handle Canada and the UK regularly, including GST/HST and VAT registrations through partners. For EU OSS filings we coordinate with a firm in Dublin.

Bring last month’s payout report. We’ll walk through it with you.

Twenty minutes, on video or phone. You’ll leave knowing what’s off in your books, even if you don’t hire us.
