FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Does Tapeline hold my funds?

No. Tapeline connects to exchanges through API keys you create. Funds stay in your exchange accounts, and we recommend keys with trading permission but no withdrawal permission.

Which exchanges are supported?

Tapeline routes across 12 venues, covering major spot and perpetual markets. The full list and status for each venue is shown in the app's connections panel.

How do the risk limits work?

Limits are checked on the client before an order is sent. If an order would breach your daily loss, position size or open-order cap, it is blocked and you see the reason on screen.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Starter plan includes unlimited paper trading, and Pro comes with a 14-day trial that needs no card to start.
