Questions

The ones founders ask us first.

If yours isn’t here, the call is free and the answer is usually short.

Ask on a call
Do you take equity instead of fees?

Only through accelerator programs we are approved to work with, and only when the program requires it. We do not ask for equity from companies we are not already working with, and we will say so in writing if you ask.

We have no revenue yet. Are we too early?

No. Most of our clients were pre-revenue when they first called. Formation and the founder agreements are the right first step, and they are cheaper to get right now than to fix after your first investor asks.

What happens if we outgrow you?

We refer you to firms we would hire ourselves, and we hand over the complete file, including drafts and correspondence, without a fee for the transfer.

Do you represent investors and founders on the same deal?

No. We act for one side of any transaction, and we tell you which side on the first call. If a conflict appears mid-matter, we say so the same day and help you find other counsel.

How do you bill the monthly retainer?

A flat fee each month with a cap you choose. You see every hour logged against it in a weekly note, and anything above the cap waits for your approval.
