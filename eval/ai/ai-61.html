2,312 metres up in the Pale di San Martino. Forty-two bunks, a wood stove, and dinner at seven. Open from 20 June to 21 September, reached only on foot.

The walk up

From the car park at Malga Ronch it's 470 metres of climbing on trail 712. The path is well marked and steady, and there's no scrambling. Allow two and a half hours with a full pack.

  1. Malga Ronch, 1,840 m

    Park at the dairy farm (€6 a day, cash). The trail leaves from behind the cheese hut, past the water trough.

  2. Bait de le Fede, 2,050 m

    After the larch wood, a shepherd's hut with a spring. Fill your bottles here; there's no water higher up until the hut.

  3. Lago Verde, 2,210 m

    A small green tarn under the north wall. The path turns to scree switchbacks for the last stretch.

  4. The hut, 2,312 m

    You'll see the red shutters a few minutes before you arrive. Leave boots in the boot room; hut slippers are by the stairs.

Staying the night

Most guests come for half-board: a bunk, a three-course dinner and breakfast. Bring a sleeping-bag liner; blankets and pillows are here.

  1. 15:00

    Check in at the bar. Apple strudel comes out of the oven about now.

  2. 19:00

    Dinner at long tables: barley soup, then canederli or polenta with mushrooms, then dessert.

  3. 22:00

    Lights out in the dorms. The stove stays lit through the night.

  4. 06:30

    Breakfast. Early starters can take a packed lunch, ordered the night before.

Early summer

Snow can lie on the last switchbacks until early July. Check with us before you set out; we'll tell you if you need microspikes.

August

Every bunk is taken on weekends. Book a few weeks ahead, or come midweek, when it's quieter.

Winter room

Six bunks, blankets and a gas stove, but no food and no heating. Bring everything you need, and close the shutters when you leave.
