No screws. No flat-pack. Nothing you can't fix.

Furniture fails at the joints. So we spend our time there.

Wedged through-tenons
The chair legs pass through the seat and are locked with a hardwood wedge. When the timber settles, the joint tightens.
Timber from within 200 km
Slow-grown pine, ash and birch from certified forests in Götaland, air-dried for at least a year before it reaches a saw.
Hard-wax oil, not lacquer
A scratch is sanded and re-oiled in ten minutes at home. We send the oil for free, and the instructions with it.
Lifetime repair
Loose joint, cracked rail, wobbling table: post it to the workshop. We repair it for the cost of the return shipping.
