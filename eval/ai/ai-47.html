A day at camp

Less lecture, more lab.

Short demos, then you're hands-on. Every day ends with something that moves, beeps or breaks (and gets fixed).

Day camp runs 9:00 am – 4:00 pm, Monday to Friday. Optional extended care from 8:00 am and until 5:30 pm.

  1. 9:00

    Boot-up

    Team check-in, a quick challenge and the day's goal.

  2. 9:30

    Mini-workshop

    Soldering, motor control, CAD, a new line of code: one skill at a time.

  3. 10:30

    Build block

    Work on your robot with mentors circulating to help.

  4. 12:00

    Lunch & lawn games

    Bring your own lunch. Snacks and water on us.

  5. 1:00

    Guest engineer

    Local engineers and makers share how they work and what they build.

  6. 2:00

    Test & iterate

    Run trials, find the bugs, fix them. Repeat.

  7. 3:30

    Demo & debrief

    Show the group what you made today.

Questions

Good to know

Does my teen need prior experience?

Nope. We group campers by comfort level, so first-timers build alongside others just getting started, and returning campers get tougher challenges.

What should they bring?

A packed lunch, water bottle, closed-toe shoes, and a laptop if they have one (loaners are available). We supply everything else.

Is it safe?

Yes. Every tool has a safety briefing, eye protection is required in the lab, and campers only use tools after a mentor sign-off. Our mentors are background-checked and trained in first aid.

Who are the mentors?

Working engineers, college robotics team members and experienced teachers, with a 6:1 camper-to-mentor ratio.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund up to 30 days before your session starts. After that, we'll transfer your spot to another session or a friend.
