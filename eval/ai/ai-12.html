Our story

Built by hand, in a converted feed barn.

We started with a homebrew kit, a borrowed pot and a lot of patience. Today we run a 15-barrel brewhouse, and we still taste every batch before it leaves the tank.

Local ingredients

Malt from a family maltster 90 miles away. Hops grown on farms within two counties when the harvest allows.

Patience in the cellar

Lagers rest for weeks. Sours sit for months. We don't rush a beer just to hit a calendar.

Packaged for the road

Cans and growlers filled fresh at the taproom, and distributed to bottle shops across the region.
