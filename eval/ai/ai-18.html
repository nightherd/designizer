Questions people ask before the first class.

Do I need any Spanish to start?

No. Our placement conversation finds your level, including if you are starting from zero. Nobody is put in a group that is too advanced for them.

Can I take the classes online?

Yes. Online groups meet on video with the same teacher and the same six-person limit as the school. You can switch between the two formats at any time.

What if my schedule changes?

Missed a class? Recordings of every group session stay available for a week, and you can make up one session per month with another group at the same level.

Is the free trial class really free?

Yes. It is a full 50-minute class in your target level, with no card required. If it is not right for you, we will tell you which school or course fits better.
