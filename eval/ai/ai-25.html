    You're the bank now. Here's what that means.

    Self-custody moves the responsibility to you. We'd rather you know exactly where the lines are before you move a single coin.

    Cairn can

    • Sign transactions on your deviceYour private keys are created and stored locally, encrypted with your passcode.
    • Warn you before you sendCairn flags new addresses, unusual fees and token approvals that can drain a wallet.
    • Work with a hardware walletPair a Ledger, Trezor or Keystone so keys never touch your phone at all.

    Cairn can't

    • Reset your recovery phraseIf you lose the twelve words and your device, nobody can restore your funds. Not us, not support.
    • See your balance or historyWe don't run accounts, so there's nothing on our side to look at, sell or hand over.
    • Reverse a transactionOnce a transfer is confirmed on the network, it's final. Check the address before you send.

    Set up in about four minutes

    Most of that time is spent writing down your phrase, and that part is worth doing slowly.

    1. Write down your phrase

      Cairn shows twelve words once. Copy them onto paper or steel, in order, and store them somewhere only you can reach.

    2. Confirm three of the words

      Cairn asks for three words at random to check your copy is right. Screenshots and clipboard copying are blocked on this screen.

    3. Receive your first coins

      Share your address or QR code. Start with a small amount, then send it back to yourself to prove the round trip works.
