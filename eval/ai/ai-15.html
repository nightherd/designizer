Buildings made slowly, and kept for a long time.

We design houses, libraries and small workplaces for people who plan to stay. Most of our clients come to us through a neighbour, a previous job, or a building they walked past and could not stop thinking about.

We draw everything ourselves before we talk about money.

01 — Practice

A small office by choice.

We take on about six projects a year, so every one of them gets a partner's attention from the first sketch to the last door handle.

The practice started in a converted printing works with a single drafting table and a borrowed plotter. We still work at that table. Models are built in the studio, not outsourced, and site visits are made by whoever drew the thing in question.

We are interested in material that ages in front of you: brick that darkens, timber that greys, plaster that shows where the hand was. We prefer details that can be repaired by a local builder with ordinary tools.

02 — Selected work

Four buildings, mostly in the valley.

P—01

Orchard Street House

Lowerfield, 2019–2021

A family house built on a sloping corner lot. The living spaces sit along the south face, and the bedrooms are pushed to the back where the neighbouring garage used to be. The roof light lets the morning in without any view of the street.

Area
212 m²
Structure
Rammed earth to 900 mm, timber above
Budget
Delivered within 4% of the tender
P—02

Mill Lane Library

Harrow Bend, 2022–2024

A branch library for a town that had lost its last one in a fire. The reading room is a single tall space with a pitched roof of glulam trusses, and the children's floor is kept low so that parents can see across it.

Area
640 m²
Structure
Glulam trusses, brick infill
Recognition
Regional Design Award, 2024
P—03

Quarry Office

Stonemere Yard, 2023

Offices for a stone merchant who wanted the building to look like it had always been part of the yard. The window module repeats across the elevation at a size the local masons could set without a crane.

Area
380 m²
Cladding
Weathering steel, reclaimed sandstone base
P—04

Ashgrove Reading Room

Ashgrove School, 2025 — in progress

A quiet room for a secondary school that has no quiet room. Furniture is arranged around a large central pivot so that the teacher can see every desk without standing. Construction completes next spring.

Area
120 m²
Status
On site, handover March
03 — Services

What we do, and how far we go.

  1. A.

    Feasibility and site study

    Surveys, planning history, a realistic budget and an honest answer about whether the building should exist at all. Usually four to six weeks.

  2. B.

    Design

    Drawings, models and material samples in our own studio. We present options early and stop when you have chosen one, not when we have run out of ideas.

  3. C.

    Contract administration

    Tender management, site visits every two weeks, and a named person on the phone when the builder has a question at 7 a.m.

  4. D.

    Repairs and alterations

    Smaller jobs on older buildings, including listed and vernacular properties. We are happy to take them on if they fit the work we are already doing.
