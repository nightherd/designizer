What we help with

Some families come to us with a diagnosis. Others just have a feeling that something isn't quite right. Either way, we'll assess properly and tell you plainly what we find.

Babies

  • Head turning to one side (torticollis)
  • Flat spots on the head
  • Late rolling, sitting or crawling
  • Low or high muscle tone
  • Hip dysplasia follow-up

Growing kids

  • Toe walking and flat feet
  • Clumsiness and coordination
  • Cerebral palsy and other neurological conditions
  • Developmental coordination disorder
  • Hypermobility

Sporty teens

  • Knee and heel pain from growth spurts
  • Sprains, strains and return to sport
  • Recovery after surgery or a fracture
  • Back and posture pain
  • Scoliosis exercise programs

Your first visit

The first appointment is 60 minutes, so nobody is rushed. Bring comfy clothes, any reports you have, and a favourite toy if it helps.

  1. Tell us what you've noticed

    We start with your story: pregnancy and birth, milestones so far, and what's worrying you now.

  2. We watch them play

    Climbing, balancing, ball games. Most children don't realise they're being assessed.

  3. We explain what we found

    You'll hear what's typical, what isn't, and what we'd suggest, in plain words and before you leave.

  4. You go home with a plan

    A few short games for home, a written summary, and a letter to your GP or school if needed.
