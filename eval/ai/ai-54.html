What happens in the 40 minutes

The van carries the tires, a torque wrench and a balancer. The car stays where you left it.

  1. 0 min

    Van pulls up beside your car

    We check the size on your sidewall and confirm the tires you asked for before we lift anything.

  2. 10 min

    Tread and pressure check

    We measure every tire and write the depth down on your receipt, so you know when to plan the next set.

  3. 25 min

    Fit and balance

    Old tires come off, new ones go on, and each wheel is balanced with weights to within a few grams.

  4. 40 min

    Torque and sign-off

    Lug nuts are set to the manufacturer's figure with a torque wrench. We check it again after 50 miles.

Is it time for new tires?

Put a penny into the main groove of the tread, head first, with Lincoln facing you.

If you can see the top of his head, the tread is under 2/32 of an inch, which is the legal minimum in most US states. Replace the tire now.

If the tread covers his head, you have some life left. Measure it with a gauge when you book, and we will tell you what we find.

Under 2/32 in (1.6 mm)
Replace now. The tire is past the legal limit.
2/32 to 4/32 in
Plan a replacement. Wet braking gets worse with every mm you lose.
Above 4/32 in
Good for now. Check again in about six months.

Questions people ask first

Do you need a space in the driveway?

No. The van needs about one car length of clear space beside the vehicle. A street spot works if it is safe to stand next to the car.

What if my car has run-flat tires or TPMS sensors?

We fit both. Run-flats need a specialist bead lubricant, which is included. Sensors are reset with a scan tool at the end of the job and the cost is listed on your quote.

Can you fit tires I bought elsewhere?

Yes, provided they match the size on your sidewall. Bring them to the van or have them delivered to the address on your booking.

What if it rains?

We fit in light rain. In heavy rain or ice we move your slot at no charge and text you the new time.
