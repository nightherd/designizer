03 / Fees

What it costs, written down before you ask.

If you are…You pay
Renting a flatNothingNo finding fee, no admin fee, no “reference check” charge. The landlord pays us.
Letting your flat1 month's rentPlus VAT, once, when a tenant signs. Optional management: 6% of monthly rent.
Selling your flat1.6%Of the sale price, on completion. If it doesn't sell, you owe us nothing — photos and floor plan included.
BuyingNothingWe work for the seller, and we'll say so. We can recommend an independent surveyor.
How a viewing works
  1. You call or send the formA real person replies the same working day, usually within two hours.
  2. We book a slot within 48 hoursEvenings until 19:30 on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Saturday mornings too.
  3. The agent who listed it shows you roundNot a stand-in. They'll point out the boiler age and the noisy neighbour if there is one.
  4. Decide in your own timeWe don't run “best and final” rounds on rentals. First complete application gets it.
