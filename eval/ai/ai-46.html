Practice areas

FAQ

Common questions

Don't see yours? Ask us directly. The consultation is free.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing upfront. Most cases are taken on contingency: our fee is a percentage of what we recover, and if there's no recovery, you owe no attorney's fee. Severance reviews are typically a modest flat fee.

How long do I have to file a claim?

Deadlines can be as short as 180 days for some agency charges, so don't wait. We'll identify every deadline that applies to you during your consultation.

I still work there. Can I talk to you?

Yes, and it's often the best time to. We can advise you on documenting issues and protecting yourself. Retaliating against you for consulting a lawyer is itself illegal.

Should I sign the severance agreement I was given?

Not before an attorney reviews it. Severance agreements usually waive your right to sue, and the first offer is rarely the best one. You may also have a legal right to time to consider it.

What should I bring to the consultation?

Anything helpful you already have: offer letter, performance reviews, pay stubs, termination notice, and relevant emails or texts. Don't take confidential company documents. We'll guide you on what's appropriate.
