Questions

Fair questions.

Something else? Write to us at hello@dueday.example — a human answers within a day.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They get an email with a link to the invoice and can pay in a couple of clicks — card, bank transfer, or wire. No sign-up, no app.

How do the fees work?

Your plan is a flat monthly price. Payments carry the standard processor fee (e.g. 2.9% + 30¢ for cards, 0.8% capped at $5 for ACH). You can choose to pass card fees on to the client.

Can I import invoices from my current tool?

Yes. Upload a CSV or connect your existing account and we'll bring over clients, invoices and payment history so your numbers stay continuous.

What happens if I cancel?

You can export everything — PDFs, CSVs, and your client list — at any time. If you downgrade, your existing invoices stay accessible and payable.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, payments are handled by PCI-DSS Level 1 processors, and we never sell or share your client information.
