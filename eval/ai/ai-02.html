Questions

Good to know.

Can I skip or pause deliveries?

Yes. Skip a single delivery, pause for up to six months, or cancel from your account page. We never charge you for a delivery you’ve skipped.

How fresh will my coffee be?

Every bag is roasted the day before it ships and carries its roast date on the label. Most orders arrive within 2 to 4 days.

Do you grind the coffee?

Whole bean is the default. If you prefer, choose a grind for espresso, filter, French press or moka pot.

Where do you ship?

Across the US today, with Canada and the UK coming soon. Shipping is free on every plan.

What if I don’t like a coffee?

Tell us within 14 days and we’ll replace it or refund it, no returns needed.
