Questions, answered.

Will other people know Scribe is recording?

Yes. Scribe announces itself in the call and participants can opt out. Desktop capture mode shows a consent prompt too.

Is my data used to train AI models?

Never. Your recordings and transcripts are encrypted and only used to serve you. Delete them any time.

Which platforms does it support?

Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and in-person meetings through our mobile app.

Can I edit the notes?

Absolutely. Edit summaries, reassign tasks, and re-run them with a different template whenever you like.
