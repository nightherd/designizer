The room

Built for listening.

Sixty seats, low light, and acoustics tuned so you can hear a brush on a snare from the back row. No TVs, no distractions — phones stay in pockets, conversation drops when the band starts.

We’ve hosted rising local talent and touring legends alike, and we pay our musicians fairly because great nights start with great players.
