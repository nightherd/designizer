The studio

Design from the ground up.

We read a site before we draw on it — its soils, its water, its light and the people who already love it. The best landscapes feel inevitable, as though they were always meant to be there.

Our team of 24 landscape architects, ecologists and horticulturists works from a single studio in Portland. We take on a small number of projects each year so every one gets the time a living thing deserves — from first walk to tenth growing season.

Native first

Over 80% of our planting palettes are regionally native, chosen for habitat value and resilience.

Water as structure

Rain gardens, bioswales and ponds that turn runoff into the backbone of the design.

Built to mature

We design for year ten, not opening day — and stay on as stewards while the landscape settles in.

What we do

Four scales, one sensibility.

From a courtyard to a watershed, our process stays the same: listen closely, design patiently, build carefully.

  • 01

    Residential gardens

    Private gardens, estates and rooftops — planted spaces made for daily life and the changing seasons.

  • 02

    Public realm

    Parks, plazas and streetscapes that invite people to linger, gather and look after one another.

  • 03

    Ecological restoration

    Wetland, riparian and meadow restoration grounded in field ecology and long-term monitoring.

  • 04

    Master planning

    Campus, institutional and mixed-use frameworks where landscape leads, not follows.

How we work

A process measured in seasons.

Listen

Site walks, soil tests, community conversations and a full year of seasonal observation where we can.

4–8 weeks

Shape

Concept and schematic design, tested with physical models and planting studies.

2–4 months

Build

Construction documents and close site supervision with growers and craftspeople we trust.

3–12 months

Steward

Three years of establishment care and monitoring, so the landscape matures as intended.
