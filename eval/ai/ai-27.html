Hosting that stays out of the way.

Most hosts are built to show you dashboards. Longwave is built to get your episode out, correctly tagged and on time, and then leave you alone to make the next one.

Your feed is ordinary RSS. If you ever leave, one export moves every episode, artwork and transcript with it.

What you get

  • Upload once. It reaches every app.

    Drop in a WAV or MP3 and Longwave writes the feed that Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube Music and everything else reads. Titles, show notes and chapter markers arrive intact.

  • Ads go where you put them.

    Mark a spot at a timestamp, set your rate, and let sponsors book it. We take a fixed share of what you sell and nothing from the audio itself.

  • Transcripts without the wait.

    Every episode gets a searchable transcript and captions the moment it publishes. Edit the text yourself if a name is spelled wrong.

  • A site that is already yours.

    Point your own domain at a finished show page with episodes, a subscribe menu and an email signup. No design work required.
