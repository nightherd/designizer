FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? Our support line is staffed by humans around the clock.

Will my car work with Fernway?

Yes, if it can DC fast charge. Every dispenser has both CCS1 and NACS cables, which covers nearly every EV sold in North America. Older CHAdeMO vehicles can use an adapter.

Why does charging slow down near 100%?

Your car limits power as the battery fills to protect it. Most cars charge fastest between 10% and 60%, then taper off. Our estimator above takes this into account.

What if a stall isn't working?

Call the number on the dispenser. If we can't fix it remotely, we'll credit your next session and dispatch a technician. Our 90-day uptime is published live on our status page.

Is the electricity really renewable?

We match 100% of the energy dispensed with wind and solar contracts in the same grid region, and 140 sites have their own solar canopies.

Can I reserve a stall?

Fernway Plus members can hold a stall for up to 30 minutes ahead of arrival. Reservations are free and release automatically if you're running late.
