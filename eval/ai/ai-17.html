Not sure how much light you have?

Low light

A north-facing window, or a room where you can read at midday without a lamp. Snake plants and ZZ plants cope well here.

Bright, indirect

Within two metres of an east or south window, where the sun doesn't hit the leaves directly. Most tropical plants prefer this.

Full sun

A south or west windowsill with several hours of direct sun. Aloe and jade plants thrive here.
