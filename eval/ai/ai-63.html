Questions owners ask first.

Does my customer need to know?

Yes, and that is intentional. Your customer receives the invoice from you with a payment link. They pay the same way they always do.

What if my customer doesn't pay?

We follow up on the due date and tell you where it stands every week. What happens next is spelled out in the agreement you sign, before any money moves.

Do you check my personal credit?

No personal guarantee is required. We underwrite on the strength of your customers' payment history and your business's receivables.
