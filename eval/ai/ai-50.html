Questions people ask

What if there are no open docks where I'm going?

The app shows live dock counts. If your station is full, tap "Find nearby" and we'll add 15 free minutes so you can ride to the next one.

Do I get a helmet?

No, but there are free loaner helmets at the ten busiest stations, and Marlow Cycles gives a $10 discount to Tern members.

What happens in winter?

We run all year. From December to February about a third of the fleet switches to studded tires, and snow-day rides are free for the first 20 minutes.

Can I reserve a bike?

Yes, for up to 10 minutes. Reservations are free for monthly members and $0.50 for everyone else.

Who runs this?

Tern is a nonprofit co-op operated under contract with the City of Marlow. Fare revenue covers about 70% of the cost, and the rest comes from the transit levy.
