The House

A townhouse, quietly reimagined.

Tsukimi began as a merchant's townhouse with a tiled roof and a courtyard garden. We kept its timber bones, its sliding shoji and its deep eaves, and fitted it with the things a modern traveller needs: heated floors, a proper bed, and silence after dark.

Every morning, our team serves breakfast in the courtyard. Every evening, the garden lamps come on, and the street falls still.

Experiences

Kyoto, at its own pace.

i.

Morning walk

An unhurried stroll through Kiyomizu-dera and the Sannenzaka slopes before the crowds arrive.

ii.

Tea ceremony

A private chado session in our tea room, led by a teacher from a Gion family of tea masters.
