First visit

Never climbed?
Perfect.

Most of our members started with zero experience. Here's how your first hour goes.

Sign the waiver

Do it online before you arrive and skip the queue. Takes two minutes.

Gear up

Rent shoes and a chalk bag at the desk. Wear something you can stretch in.

Join an intro

A 20‑minute walkthrough on falling, spotting and wall etiquette. Runs on the hour, free.

Climb yellow

Start on the yellow circuit, rest between tries, and ask anyone for beta. Really, anyone.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to book?

Not for general climbing — just turn up. Intro sessions are first-come, first-served, and kids club and courses need booking.

What should I wear?

Anything you can move in. Skip jeans if you can. Climbing shoes are required on the wall — rent them at the desk if you don't have your own.

Is bouldering safe?

Our walls max out at 4.5m and sit over 40cm of continuous padded flooring. The intro covers how to fall and land properly — the most important skill you'll learn.

Can kids climb?

Yes! Ages 6+ are welcome with a supervising adult, and our Saturday Kids Club is the best way to get them started.

Is there parking?

Free parking for 60 cars behind the building, plenty of bike racks, and the 14 bus stops right outside.
