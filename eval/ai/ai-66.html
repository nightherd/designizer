The process

Four steps, no shortcuts

Every piece passes through the same hands from hide to finished edge. Nothing is machine-stamped or rushed.

01

Select

We choose each hide by hand, rejecting any with scarring or uneven grain.

02

Cut

Patterns are drawn and cut by hand so the grain runs in the strongest direction.

03

Stitch

Saddle-stitched with waxed linen thread, two needles, every hole pricked by awl.

04

Finish

Edges burnished and conditioned with beeswax. Stamped with our mark, then inspected.

Our story

A workbench, a window, and a stubborn idea.

"A wallet should look better on its fifth year than its first."

Tannery Row started in a rented room above a shoe repair shop with one stitching pony and a stack of hides from a family tannery two hours north. We still work from that same kind of bench, just with a bigger window.

We make a small number of pieces each month, so every order gets full attention. If something ever fails, send it back and we'll repair it for free.

Daniel Okafor Founder & saddler
