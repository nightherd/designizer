04 / Questions

Before you
come in.

Do I need special socks?

Yes, grip socks are required on every trampoline. Bring your own or buy a pair at the desk for $3. Regular socks and bare feet are not allowed.

How young is too young?

Babies who can't yet walk are welcome in the Tot lot with a parent but not on the main floor. Anyone under 18 needs a waiver signed by a parent or guardian, which you can do online in about two minutes.

Is there a weight or height limit?

Our trampolines are rated to 250 lb per jumper. For wall runs and the Ninja strip there's a height minimum posted at each entrance.

Can I bring my own food?

Not into the park, but the party rooms and the lobby tables are fair game. Our snack bar sells pizza by the slice, hot pretzels, and a coffee that's better than it needs to be.

What if I get hurt?

Every floor has trained staff, and a first aid kit is behind the desk. Most injuries here are bruised egos. We'd still rather you tell us and sit out.
