01 — How it works

Three steps, and two of them are on us.

1

Pick the litter

Clay, pine, walnut or tofu. If you're not sure, order a sample pack first — four small bags, $6, shipping included.

2

We do the math

One cat on clumping clay goes through roughly 14 lb every four weeks. We size the box to your household so you're not stockpiling or scraping the bottom.

3

It shows up

We text you two days before it ships. Too soon? Reply "later" and it moves back a week. That's it.

05 — Questions
What if my cat refuses the new litter?

It happens a lot. Email us within 30 days of your first box and we'll swap it for a different type, free. Mixing the new litter into the old one over a week usually helps too.

How do I skip or pause?

From your account, or by replying to the shipping text. You can push a box back by a week, skip one entirely, or pause indefinitely. No fees for any of it.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere in the contiguous US. Alaska and Hawaii are on the list but freight for heavy bags is still brutal, so not yet.

Is shipping really free?

Yes, on every subscription box, whatever size it is. One-off orders like the sample pack have it built into the price.

Can I mix litter types in one box?

Yes — after your first delivery you can split a box between two litters. Handy if you've got one cat who won't touch pine.
