Weddings in Tuscan light

Wedding photography in Val d'Orcia, Chianti and the hills between. Based near Pienza, travelling across Italy.

A slow day, photographed quietly

I photograph weddings the way they unfold: the nervous laugh before the vows, your grandmother adjusting your collar, the long table at dusk.

Most of my couples marry between May and October, in villas, vineyards and stone chapels around Siena and Florence. I work alone or with one assistant, shoot in natural light, and deliver a finished gallery of 600 to 800 images within six weeks.

You will get a call from me about six weeks before your date. We walk through the schedule and find where the light is best.
